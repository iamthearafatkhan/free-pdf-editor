Free PDF Editor — Edit Text in Any PDF Online

How to use

1
Open your PDFClick "Open PDF" or drag a file onto the page.
2
Click any paragraphIt becomes editable like in Word.
3
Type or deleteThe text reflows automatically — remaining words slide to fill the gap.
4
Preview & saveClick "Save as PDF" — preview first, then download.
