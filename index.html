How to use

1
Open your PDFClick "Open PDF" or drag a file onto the page.
2
Click any line of textEach line is independently editable.
3
Type or deleteText after the edit slides left/right automatically.
4
Preview & saveClick "Save as PDF" — preview first, then download.

Tips

· Every line is its own box. Original alignment is preserved automatically.

· Change alignment with the floating toolbar or Ctrl+Shift+L/E/R/J.

· Double-click empty space to add a new line.
